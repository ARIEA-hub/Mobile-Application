# Closed test kit (12 testers, 14 days)

Google requires this for new personal developer accounts before production. Nobody can recruit the testers
for you; this kit makes it quick.

## Setup

1. Create a Google Group (groups.google.com) named e.g. `alarmboss-testers`, set it so anyone can join
   with approval, or collect Gmail addresses in a sheet.
2. Play Console → *Testing → Closed testing → Create track* → upload the signed `.aab` → *Testers* → add
   the Google Group email (or paste the Gmail list) → save → copy the **opt-in link**.
3. Send testers the message below. **Aim for 15–20 sign-ups**: some drop out, and you need 12 opted in for
   14 continuous days.
4. Day 14+: Play Console → *Dashboard → Apply for production* → answer the questions (testers' feedback,
   what you changed).

## Message to testers

> Hi! I'm testing my alarm app **AlarmBoss** before it goes on Google Play and need 12 people to install it
> for two weeks. It takes 2 minutes:
>
> 1. Join the group: <GROUP LINK>
> 2. Open this link on your Android phone and tap **Become a tester**: <OPT-IN LINK>
> 3. Install the app from the Play Store link on that page, and **keep it installed for 14 days**.
>
> The app needs **Android 16 or newer**. Please set a real alarm once or twice and tell me if anything
> breaks: amsampat1@gmail.com. Thank you!

## What testers should try

- Create an alarm, pick a difficulty, let it ring and complete the challenge.
- Leave it installed; open it a few times during the 14 days (Google counts engagement).
- Report crashes, late alarms or confusing screens by email.

## Rules

- Testers must use real Gmail accounts and real devices; do not use fake accounts or pay for installs
  (that breaches Play policy and can get the developer account banned).
- Keep the test running without gaps; removing testers can reset the 14-day count.
