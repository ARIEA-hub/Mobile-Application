## Full description (max 4000)

```
Tired of hitting snooze and going straight back to sleep? AlarmBoss is an alarm clock that makes you actually wake up.

Pick how strict each alarm is:

• EASY – a simple one-tap dismiss, with an optional snooze.
• MEDIUM – solve a wake-up challenge to dismiss it: quick math, read a passage aloud, sing a lyric, scan a barcode you registered, trace a pattern, unscramble a word, or walk a few steps.
• STRICT – a short workout counted by your camera (squats or jumping jacks), or a memory grid or maze if you would rather use your brain.

STAYS AWAKE WITH YOU
• Wake-up check: after a Medium or Strict alarm, it rings again a few minutes later with a quick question, so passing the task and falling back to sleep does not work.
• Optional backup alarm that rings again if you have not finished.
• Repeating alarms that really repeat, pinned-date alarms, one-tap Weekdays / Weekends / Every day.
• A two-hour lock so you cannot talk yourself out of tomorrow's alarm, with a deliberately slow emergency exit for when something is genuinely wrong.
• Alarms come back by themselves after a restart, an app update, or a time-zone change.

MAKE MORNINGS A GAME
• Earn XP, level up, build a wake-up streak and unlock avatars.
• Every seventh day of a streak brings a three-stage boss fight.
• A stats tab with your records and a four-week wake-up calendar.
• Challenges get harder as you improve.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE
• Gradual volume fade-in, adjustable loudness, and an option to raise a turned-down phone for the alarm.
• Silent mode with vibration and a flashing camera light.
• A puzzle alternative to the workout, for anyone who cannot do it.
• Follows your 12/24-hour clock setting, in light, dark or system theme.
• Optional holiday skipping: it asks you before skipping any alarm, and nothing is ever skipped without your yes.

PARENT MODE
Set a school-morning alarm for your child and lock alarms behind your 4-digit PIN, so they cannot be changed or switched off without you.

SLEEP COUNSELOR
Chat about caffeine, screens, naps, light, insomnia, shift work and more. It works entirely on your phone, with no account and no internet. You can optionally add your own Google Gemini key for fuller answers.

YOUR PRIVACY
No ads. No account. No tracking. Your alarms and progress stay on your phone. The camera is used only on the spot for scanning and exercise counting, and nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Sleep tips in the app are general wellness information, not medical advice. If you are worried about your sleep or health, please see a doctor.
```
